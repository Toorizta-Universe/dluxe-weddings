The Studio.

A small practice, led by one person who has done this for nearly a decade.

Founder

Madelyn Sánchez.

International certified Wedding Planner. Bilingual officiant. Master of Ceremonies.

D'Luxe Events is led by Madelyn Sánchez, internationally certified Wedding Planner trained by INIBEP, a globally recognized institution in the event planning industry. With more than eight years of practice in the wedding industry, she is also a certified Master of Ceremonies and bilingual officiant for symbolic ceremonies, allowing her to guide and oversee every moment of the celebration with professionalism and elegance.

The studio is intentionally small. Madelyn is the one who answers your first message, sits across from you at the consultation, holds your bouquet for you before you walk down the aisle, and stays until the last guest is gone.

INIBEP · San José · June 2025 Internationally certified Wedding & Event Planner — Instituto Iberoamericano. Registration No. 18765.
Philosophy

No two celebrations should look alike.

We do not offer predefined packages. Every couple deserves a celebration designed exclusively for them.

At D'Luxe Events, we believe every wedding is a unique celebration that deserves to be designed with elegance, sensitivity, and exceptional attention to detail. Our work is to transform your vision into an extraordinary experience — one that reflects your essence and remains unforgettable for everyone involved.

That belief shapes everything: the way we listen during the first call, the vendors we choose, the language we use in your ceremony, the small surprises we save for you on the day. Designed exclusively is not a tagline. It is the working principle.
